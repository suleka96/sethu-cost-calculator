Wax & chemicals

Wax: ~180g/batch (20g/m over a 9m batch) — Super Voil split ~50% paraffin / 35% bee wax / 15% gum rosin, Modal Silk paraffin‑only — at sheet prices. Chemicals: ~300g salt + 400ml soaping + 80g soda + 150ml acetic + 300ml soaping (2nd stage) + 250ml fixing per batch, also at sheet prices. Both are estimates — adjust either to match what you actually use.
