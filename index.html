After-treatment

Dye

Per yard: 4 mg urea, 20 ml sodium silicate. Dye follows the sheet's urea : dye = 2 : 6 ratio — dye is 3× the urea weight. Java batik doesn't depend on fabric type — only the yards set above drive these amounts.

Recipe

Batch

Everything on this tab is batch-level: wax, the water and gas to boil it off, then soaping, fixing and fabric softener applied to the whole batch at once — all set once per batch, then scaled down by fabric used ÷ batch size, using the fabric used (yards) from Product Cost.

Wax

The wax blend — and its cost — depends on the fabric type set above: ~180g/batch (20g/yd over a 9yd batch). Super Voil & Cotton split ~50% paraffin / 35% bee wax / 15% gum rosin (Rs 800 / 2,000 / 1,000 per kg); Modal Silk & Rayon are paraffin‑only (Rs 800/kg).

Utility — boiling the wax off (gas + water)

Sheet prices gas (Rs 500) & water (Rs 60) per production batch, for boiling the wax off — scaled by fabric used ÷ batch size. Equipment (junting, funnel, gloves, brush) is a one‑time tool cost, so it's left out of the per‑item price.

Soaping, fixing & softener

Assumption — 10 ml water per 3 yd

Water needed scales with fabric used (yards): 3 yd → 10 ml, 6 yd → 20 ml, and so on; soaping, fixing and fabric softener are each dosed at 2× that water figure, priced at the sheet's Rs 550/L, Rs 700/L and Rs 1,200/L.
